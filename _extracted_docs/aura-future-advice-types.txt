Aura Future Advice Types
Google Docs-ready version
Future updates should include:
Entrepreneur: Advice for entrepreneurs who are just getting started. The advice should come from an experienced, successful, multi-millionaire who has successfully built and run multiple complex businesses in various sectors and is now retired. This individual should now be mentoring people and helping people build businesses from ideation to their first revenue streams, then to scaling multi-million-dollar businesses. [cite:22]
Teacher: Should teach advice that helps students study for tests and exams. The teacher should be experienced. The teacher will learn the user’s learning style and tailor the learning advice according to their comprehension level, pace, and preferred study style. [cite:22]
Career Advice: Should help users navigate job choices, career transitions, promotions, layoffs, workplace politics, salary negotiations, resume improvements, interview preparation, and long-term career planning. It should adapt to whether the user is a student, early-career professional, mid-career professional, executive, or someone starting over. [cite:22]
Legal Advice: Should help users better understand common legal situations, documents, risks, and next steps in plain language. It can help with contracts, landlord-tenant issues, small claims, employment issues, business formation, intellectual property basics, consumer rights, and legal document preparation support, while making clear when a licensed attorney is needed. [cite:22]
Health Advice: Should provide practical, easy-to-understand wellness guidance for everyday health concerns, lifestyle improvements, nutrition, sleep, fitness, stress, and prevention. It should help users understand symptoms, healthy habits, and possible next steps, while clearly encouraging professional medical care for emergencies, diagnoses, prescriptions, and serious conditions. [cite:22]
Relationship Advice: Should help users with dating, communication, trust, boundaries, conflict resolution, emotional compatibility, breakups, reconciliation, marriage concerns, and family relationship dynamics. It should understand both emotional nuance and practical next steps.
Mental Wellness Advice: Should help users manage stress, burnout, anxiety, overthinking, confidence issues, loneliness, motivation, emotional regulation, and life pressure in a supportive way. It should offer grounded coping strategies, reflection prompts, and habit-based improvement while clearly distinguishing coaching from therapy or crisis care.
Financial Advice: Should help users budget, save money, reduce debt, improve credit, plan for emergencies, invest carefully, compare financial choices, and set financial goals. It should also help users understand taxes, side income, and basic wealth-building strategies in language that is easy to follow.
Parenting Advice: Should help parents navigate discipline, communication, routines, child development, school concerns, co-parenting, and emotional support for children at different ages. It should be practical, calm, and age-aware.
Life Coaching: Should help users make decisions, set goals, stay accountable, build confidence, improve habits, and create structure in their lives. This would be useful for people who feel stuck, overwhelmed, or unsure about what to do next.
Spiritual Advice: Should help users explore faith, purpose, meaning, prayer, discipline, doubt, moral questions, and personal growth through a spiritual lens. It could be personalized based on the user’s beliefs or values.
Productivity Advice: Should help users manage time, reduce procrastination, build routines, improve focus, organize tasks, and finish what they start.
Business Strategy Advice: Should help business owners with positioning, pricing, offers, customer acquisition, branding, operations, partnerships, retention, and growth strategy.
Startup Fundraising Advice: Should help founders prepare for pitch decks, investor outreach, fundraising strategy, traction storytelling, valuation conversations, and understanding what investors look for.
Marketing Advice: Should help users grow visibility through branding, content strategy, social media, paid ads, email marketing, funnels, SEO, community growth, and customer psychology.
Sales Advice: Should help users improve persuasion, objection handling, outreach, closing, follow-up, lead qualification, and relationship-based selling.
Technology Advice: Should help users make decisions about software, hardware, platforms, tools, integrations, automation, digital workflows, and implementation choices.
Cybersecurity Advice: Should help users understand online safety, phishing, password security, privacy risks, identity protection, device security, business cyber hygiene, and basic incident response.
Developer/Programming Advice: Should help users debug code, choose tech stacks, structure projects, understand concepts, improve code quality, and build apps faster.
UI/UX Advice: Should help users improve usability, product flows, accessibility, wireframes, conversion design, layout decisions, and design system consistency.
Student Advice: Should help with school-life balance, motivation, assignments, studying, academic planning, class performance, test prep, and managing deadlines.
Travel Advice: Should help users plan trips, compare destinations, build itineraries, manage budgets, understand travel logistics, and choose experiences based on their preferences and budget.
Fitness Advice: Should help users with workout planning, fat loss, muscle gain, endurance, recovery, mobility, consistency, and realistic goal tracking.
Nutrition Advice: Should help users improve eating habits, meal planning, calorie awareness, performance nutrition, healthy grocery choices, and sustainable lifestyle changes without being overly restrictive.
Fashion/Style Advice: Should help users improve personal style, outfit choices, grooming, confidence, event dressing, and shopping decisions based on budget, body type, goals, and aesthetic preference.
Home/Living Advice: Should help users with renting, moving, organization, furniture decisions, roommate issues, home setup, smart home tools, and making their environment more functional and comfortable.

Suggested rollout strategy
Launch with a small number of highly useful categories instead of too many at once.
Give each advice type a clear persona, tone, and use case.
Build trust-heavy categories carefully, especially health, legal, mental wellness, and financial advice. [cite:22]
Let users choose between quick advice, deep guidance, and action-plan mode.
Add personalization so Aura adapts to a user’s goals, behavior, communication style, and stage of life.
